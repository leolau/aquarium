Aquarium · final icon & loader: AI · Circuit with Gemini stars

A self-contained ecosystem (coral fish, jellyfish, amber snail, seaweed, bubbles) where one seaweed frond grows as circuit traces ending in three Gemini-style AI stars: smooth, concave sides and a #5b8cff → #a78bfa gradient with a hint of #f0abfc.

In the loader, each star in turn swells (scale 0.6 → 1.25, no rotation) while a white core flashes, a soft halo blooms outward and fades, and a faint four-ray flare glints. The order runs left → top → right over a 2.4 s loop. With reduced motion it becomes a gentle opacity pulse.

Inline SVG + CSS only. The water follows --color-accent, all ids are prefixed aq-, and fine details drop out below 32 px (.aq-sm wrapper, or a media query when the SVG is used as an image). The favicon is redrawn for 16 px: bowl, fish and one circuit frond with a Gemini star.
